--- layout: home paginate: true alt_title: "WISDOM" sub_title: "mm-Wave Interferometric Survey of Dark Object Masses" image: /assets/images/WISDOMbanner_thin.png introduction: | The WISDOM (mm-Wave Interferometric Survey of Dark Object Masses) project is making very high resolution observations of nearby galaxy centres with several telescopes, including ALMA, CFHT and the VLA. We aim to: - Develop and calibrate the use of molecular gas as a tracer for measuring supermassive black hole masses - Use these advances to tie down SMBH-host relations across the Hubble sequence with a single technique. - Explore how the evolution and properties of molecular clouds are mediated by the extreme environments present in galaxy centres. - Investigate how cloud-scale ISM properties vary in different galactic environments of galaxy centres (nuclear discs, spiral arms, bar and gas flows). - Explore the extreme environments present in galaxy centres, and the gas flows that fuel AGN - Understand the physical processes regulating star formation within individual GMCs across the Hubble sequence, which in turn impacts our understanding of the formation and evolution of galaxies in general. ---

Our Science

Supermassive Black Holes

Developing and calibrating molecular gas as a tracer for measuring supermassive black hole masses, and using these measurements to constrain SMBH-host galaxy relations across the Hubble sequence with a single technique.

Molecular Clouds & Star Formation

Exploring how the evolution and properties of molecular clouds are mediated by the extreme environments of galaxy centres, and how the physical processes regulating star formation within individual GMCs vary across the Hubble sequence, which in turn impacts our understanding of the formation and evolution of galaxies in general.

ISM, Gas Flows & Nuclear Environments

Investigating how cloud-scale ISM properties vary between nuclear discs, spiral arms, bars and gas flows, and exploring the extreme environments of galaxy centres and the gas flows that fuel AGN.
