mm-Wave Interferometric Survey of Dark Object Masses

WISDOM is a collaborative astronomical survey combining high-resolution observations from facilities including ALMA, CFHT and the VLA to investigate the molecular gas, dynamics, and evolution of nearby galaxies across the Hubble sequence.

Our Science

Supermassive Black Holes

Developing and calibrating molecular gas as a tracer for measuring supermassive black hole masses, and using these measurements to constrain SMBH-host galaxy relations across the Hubble sequence with a single technique.

Molecular Clouds & Star Formation

Exploring how the evolution and properties of molecular clouds are mediated by the extreme environments of galaxy centres, and how the physical processes regulating star formation within individual GMCs vary across the Hubble sequence, which in turn impacts our understanding of the formation and evolution of galaxies in general.

ISM, Gas Flows & Nuclear Environments

Investigating how cloud-scale ISM properties vary between nuclear discs, spiral arms, bars and gas flows, and exploring the extreme environments of galaxy centres and the gas flows that fuel AGN.
